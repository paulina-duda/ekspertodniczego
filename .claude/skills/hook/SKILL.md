# Writing the text

Three separate pieces of text, three different jobs. Do not let them say the
same thing.

## The hook

One line, at most two, centred on the frame above the data block.

**It states the paradox and never explains it.** The explanation is the
caption's job; the hook's job is to make the payoff into a question.

Shipped examples, and what makes each work:

| Hook | Why it works |
| --- | --- |
| *A tree branches. A fungus branches back.* | two clauses, second one turns the first over |
| *The only kind of growth that does not grow* | contradiction in four words |
| *Nothing in the rule says spiral.* | names the thing you are looking at, denies its cause |
| *Every one of these was stable on its own.* | past tense does the work — implies what happened next |
| *Everything at the top has one ancestor.* | plain statement of a fact the picture proves |
| *No brain. One cell. Still finds a way.* | three beats, the third is the turn |

Rules:

- **Under ~46 characters per line** (Plex Mono 34 px against a 952 px limit).
  Two lines is the maximum, and the second line has to earn itself.
- **Never restate the data block.** If the block says "found by search", the
  hook does not get to be about searching.
- **No exclamation, no rhetorical question, no "imagine if".**
- A thesis instead of a paradox is allowed but changes the voice — `affinity`
  ships *"Being alive is a matter of organization, / so it can be built from
  numbers"*, which reads as a manifesto next to the others. Deliberate there;
  do not drift into it by default.

## The data block

Three or four lines, bottom-left, small. What the thing *is*, in the flattest
possible register:

1. what field / what model — `slime mould transport network`,
   `Lenia · continuous automaton (Chan 2019)`
2. the rule as a verb list — `sense · turn · move · deposit`,
   `fire · refract · recover`
3. the number or the fact that closes it — `600,000 agents · two species`,
   `16 numbers are enough to make them chase`

Citations go here, in-frame, not in the caption. Separator is `·` with spaces.

## The caption

What it is, what the surprise is, and why it is true — in that order. Written
per reel, **both English and Polish**.

- Open on the thing itself, not on a wind-up. No "have you ever wondered".
- The surprise has to be the actual mechanism, stated plainly. If the piece is
  about anastomosis, say what anastomosis does.
- **Light cynicism only where it does not shade the science.** If it would come
  out limp, neutral is better.
- End on the fact that makes it true rather than on a flourish.
- Credit where it is owed — `yuruyurau` for `cosine-creature`, Chan for Lenia.
  If the credit is already in-frame in the data block, the caption does not
  need to repeat it.
- Offer **two versions when it is a close call**, and say which one you would
  post and why.

The Polish version is a real translation, not a gloss: it should read as if it
were written in Polish, with the same rhythm and the same restraint. Keep
technical terms that a Polish reader in the field would use (`anastomoza`,
`soliton`), and do not soften a mechanism into a metaphor to make it flow.

## Where things get filed

The hook goes into the edition's renderer, in the piece's `EDITIONS` entry.
Published hooks and captions are logged in `INSTAGRAM.md`.
